    What the real contract does that people get wrong

    • Only the sender is blacklist-checked; blacklisted addresses can still receive.
    • There's no balance require; SafeMath.sub throws on underflow.
    • transfer returns nothing, not bool.
    • deprecate forwards calls; it doesn't block them. pause is the emergency stop.
    • Ownership transfer is single-step; there is no claimOwnership.
    • Fees, when on, are credited to the owner, capped by hardcoded ceilings in setParams.
